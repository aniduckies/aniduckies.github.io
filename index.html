The channel

AniDuckies makes short videos about anime lore, starting with The Apothecary Diaries: hidden reasons, character histories and what happens later in the light novels.

AniDuckies Shorts (the app)

AniDuckies Shorts is a private production tool used only by the channel's owner. It reads a planning spreadsheet in the owner's Google Drive, prepares draft Shorts, stores working files in the owner's Drive, and uploads videos to the AniDuckies YouTube channel only after the owner approves each one.

It is not offered to the public and no one else can sign in to it.
