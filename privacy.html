Privacy Policy

AniDuckies Shorts · Effective 12 October 2026

AniDuckies Shorts ("the app") is a private tool that the owner of the AniDuckies YouTube channel uses to plan, prepare and publish the channel's own Short videos. It is not available to the public: the only Google account that signs in to it is the channel owner's.

Google data the app accesses

  • Google Sheets - reads and updates the owner's planning spreadsheet (video ideas, scripts, status).
  • Google Drive - reads media files the owner places in the app's folders, and creates and updates the app's own working files (drafts, previews) in the owner's Drive.
  • YouTube - uploads videos the owner has approved to the owner's channel, and reads the channel's video statistics (views, likes) to report performance back to the owner.

The app does not access Gmail, contacts, calendars, or any data belonging to anyone other than the channel owner.

How the data is used

Data is used only to produce and publish the channel's videos and to report their performance to the owner. It is not sold, not used for advertising, and not shared with anyone except the services that perform the work listed below.

Services that process data for the app

  • Railway - runs the app on a schedule.
  • Anthropic (Claude API) - writes draft scripts from the video topic and public research sources.
  • ElevenLabs - turns approved script text into narration audio.
  • Discord - sends status notifications to the owner.

Only the text needed for each task is sent to these services. Google account data, such as sign-in tokens, is never sent to them.

Storage and retention

Working files are kept in the owner's own Google Drive and Sheet, and can be deleted by the owner at any time. Sign-in tokens are kept only on the owner's computer and in the hosting service's private settings, and are deleted when the app is shut down.

Google API Services User Data Policy

AniDuckies Shorts' use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Revoking access

Access can be removed at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
